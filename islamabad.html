Islamabad

A planned capital city with wide roads and trees, set against the Margalla Hills. It is calm, green and a good place to rest between trips north.

Region
Islamabad Capital Territory
Elevation
About 540 m
Best time
March to May, September to November
Getting there
Islamabad International Airport, the main gateway for the north

What to see

Faisal Mosque has a tent-like roof and room for a very large congregation. The Margalla Hills have hiking trails with views from Daman-e-Koh over the city.

Saidpur Village is an old village converted into restaurants and craft shops. Lok Virsa museum covers the folk cultures of every province.

When to go

Spring and autumn are mild. Monsoon rain arrives in July and August.
