Language and music

Urdu is the national language and English is widely used in business and travel. Punjabi, Pashto, Sindhi, Balochi and Saraiki are spoken by millions. Qawwali, devotional Sufi music, is performed at shrines, most famously associated with Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan.

Art and festivals

Truck art covers lorries and buses in painted flowers, birds and calligraphy, and has become a symbol of the country. Eid, Independence Day on 14 August and Pakistan Day on 23 March bring families and flags into the streets.

The Kalash valleys of Bumburet, Rumbur and Birir in Chitral hold spring and winter festivals. The Shandur Polo Festival in July has teams from Chitral and Gilgit playing on one of the highest polo grounds in the world.
