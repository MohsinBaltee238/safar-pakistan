When to go

Mountains: April to October. Plains and Lahore: October to March. The coast: November to February. Plan around monsoon rain in July and August.

Getting around

Domestic flights connect Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi to Gilgit and Skardu, but mountain flights depend on weather. Intercity coaches are comfortable and run between major cities. The Karakoram Highway links Islamabad to Hunza and takes a day or more. Trains cover long routes slowly and cheaply.

Money and safety

The currency is the Pakistani rupee. Cards work in cities, but carry cash in the mountains. Read your government’s travel advice before booking, register with your embassy if offered, and check whether any region you plan to visit needs a permit or has restrictions. Local guides know road and weather conditions better than any website.
