Swat Valley

Green hills, a fast river and some of Pakistan’s oldest Buddhist sites. Swat is easy to reach from Islamabad and is often called the Switzerland of the East.

Region
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
Base
Mingora or Kalam
Best time
March to October
Getting there
About five hours by road from Islamabad

What to see

Kalam and the upper valley have pine forests and cold rivers. A jeep road leads on to Mahodand Lake, surrounded by meadows.

Malam Jabba has a chairlift and, in winter, snow. Near Mingora, the Butkara stupa and other ruins come from the Gandhara period, when Buddhism flourished here.

When to go

March to October for green valleys. Winter brings snow at Malam Jabba but closes some upper roads.
