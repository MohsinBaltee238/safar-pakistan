Skardu

The main town of Baltistan and the starting point for expeditions to K2, the world’s second highest mountain at 8,611 metres. Even if you never trek, the lakes and plateaus are worth the journey.

Region
Gilgit-Baltistan
Elevation
About 2,230 m
Best time
May to September
Getting there
Flights from Islamabad when weather allows, or two long days by road

What to see

Deosai National Park is a high plateau that averages about 4,100 metres. It is covered in wildflowers in July and August and has no trees at all.

Lower Kachura Lake sits beside Shangrila Resort. Shigar Fort, a restored palace, is a short drive away, and the Katpana cold desert has dunes with mountains behind them.

When to go

Roads to Deosai open in early summer, usually June, and close with snow. Autumn is quiet and cold.
