From the Khunjerab Pass to the Arabian Sea

Pakistan drops from 4,693 metres on the Chinese border to the shores of Karachi. Along the way you pass glaciers, Buddhist monasteries, Mughal gardens and some of the best street food in Asia.

Mountains in the north

Hunza, Skardu and Swat are home to valleys carved by the Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush ranges. Come between April and October for open roads, blossom and clear peaks.

Start with Hunza

Cities in the plains

Lahore holds the Walled City and Badshahi Mosque. Islamabad sits below the Margalla Hills. Karachi is a port city of beaches, bazaars and late dinners.

Start with Lahore

Culture everywhere

Five thousand years of history, from Mohenjo-daro to truck art, shows up in every region. Read about it before you go, and eat before you read.
