Food

Every province has its own cooking. Plan meals around the dishes below and ask locals where they eat, not where tourists do.

Breakfast
Halwa puri with chole, or paratha and omelette
Street food
Chaat, golgappay, seekh kebab
Drink
Chai, lassi, fresh sugarcane juice
Tip
Pick busy stalls and ask for freshly cooked food

Dishes by region

Karachi is known for biryani and nihari. Lahore has halwa puri for breakfast, paye in the morning and karahi at night. Peshawar is famous for chapli kebab and for charsi tikka cooked over charcoal.

In Balochistan, sajji is a whole chicken or lamb roasted slowly and eaten with rice. In the north, try walnut bread, apricot oil and soup made with local herbs.

Drinks and sweets

Doodh patti chai is strong and milky. Lassi, sweet or salted, is served cold in Punjab. Sugarcane juice is pressed on the street, and mango season in summer is a national event. Finish with gulab jamun or kheer.
