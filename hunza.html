Hunza Valley

A green valley under 7,000 metre peaks, reached by the Karakoram Highway. Apricot orchards, old forts and the road to China make it Pakistan’s best-known mountain trip.

Region
Gilgit-Baltistan
Base
Karimabad
Best time
April to October
Getting there
Fly to Gilgit, then drive, or take the Karakoram Highway from Islamabad

What to see

Baltit Fort above Karimabad is centuries old and has been restored as a museum. Altit Fort, nearby, is older still and sits above terraced fields.

Attabad Lake appeared after a landslide in 2010. Its turquoise water is now crossed by boat. Further north, the Passu Cones rise above the Hussaini suspension bridge, and the highway ends at the Khunjerab Pass, the border with China.

When to go

Apricot blossom arrives in April and autumn colour in October. Summer is busy and clear. Winter closes the high road and many guesthouses.
