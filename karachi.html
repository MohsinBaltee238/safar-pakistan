Karachi

Pakistan’s largest city and main port. It is loud, busy and relaxed at the same time, with a long coastline and the country’s most varied food.

Region
Sindh
Elevation
Sea level
Best time
November to February
Getting there
Jinnah International Airport, trains, buses and the national highway network

What to see

Mohatta Palace is a pink-stone mansion now used as a museum. Frere Hall and the Quaid-e-Azam Mausoleum show colonial and modern architecture.

Beaches such as Clifton and Hawks Bay are for sunsets and walks. For dinner, go to Burns Road for nihari and kebabs, or to the Port Grand area for a waterfront meal.

When to go

November to February is mild. Spring and summer are humid, with sea breezes in the evening.
