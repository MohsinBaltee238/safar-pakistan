Lahore

The cultural capital of Pakistan. Mughal monuments, narrow bazaars and food that goes on until midnight sit within a few kilometres of each other.

Region
Punjab
Elevation
About 217 m
Best time
October to March
Getting there
Allama Iqbal International Airport, trains and motorway from Islamabad

What to see

Badshahi Mosque, finished in 1673, is one of the largest mosques in the world. Across the lawn, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Walk the Walled City through Delhi Gate to the Wazir Khan Mosque, covered in tilework from the 1630s. In the evening, eat in Gawalmandi or Food Street near the fort.

When to go

October to March is cool. Summer is very hot and best spent indoors from noon.
